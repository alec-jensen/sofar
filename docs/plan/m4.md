# M4 income, bills, subscriptions, recurrence

Spec: S3 (boundary attribution), S4, S5, S6, S14, S19.9 (payment plans), S2 (extra decomposition). Architecture: A4 (`recurrence`). Engine semantics: sections 2 and 3, and 4g (bills and subscriptions), A-004, A-010.

**Goal:** a schedule engine for any interval or calendar rule, detection and "mark as recurring", income classification, bills, subscriptions, sinking envelopes, payment plans, and forecasts. All of it feeds `MonthInputs` lines and income.

**Exit criteria:**
- Schedules of any interval behave, including DST.
- A three-paycheck month is labeled and flows to the pot (example A labels).
- Invariant 8 passes.

---

### [ ] M4.1 schedule model (`internal/recurrence/schedule.go`)
- `Schedule{ Kind: interval | calendar; Interval time.Duration or Days int; Calendar CalendarRule; Anchor; GraceOverride; EffectiveFrom civil.Date; Tolerance }`.
- **Interval:** any positive duration. Day multiples use home-time calendar arithmetic; hour and minute intervals use absolute time (S14, so there is no DST drift).
- **Calendar rules:** monthly on day N (31 clamps), semi-monthly (1st and 15th, configurable), nth weekday, last business day (weekends only; holidays are a later setting).
- **Anchoring:** interval schedules re-anchor to the last observed occurrence; calendar rules keep their anchor.
- **Grace:** 25% of the interval, capped at 3 days, editable (S14, S5).
- **Effective dating:** a schedule change applies from its date (S14).
- **Split deposits:** two deposits from the same stream within tolerance count as one occurrence (S14).
- `Occurrences(s, from, to) []Occurrence` and `ExpectedCount(s, month) int`.
- Tests: `TestS14_Interval7Hours`, `TestS14_Interval13Days`, `TestS14_Interval3Weeks`, `TestS14_MonthlyDay31Clamps`, `TestS14_SemiMonthly`, `TestS14_NthWeekday`, `TestS14_LastBusinessDay`, `TestS14_DSTDoesNotDriftDayIntervals`, `TestS14_HourIntervalsUseAbsoluteTime`, `TestS14_ReanchorOnObserved`, `TestS14_EffectiveDatedChange`, `TestS14_SplitDepositsOneOccurrence`.

### [ ] M4.2 detection and "mark as recurring"
- Detection (S14): group by normalized merchant (M6 provides normalization; until then use the ported `Prettify` plus lowercasing), infer the interval from the **median gap**, and require the configurable threshold (default 3) and amount consistency. Known monthly merchant kinds (subscriptions, phone, utilities, insurance, rent, loans, gyms) may be suggested after one charge (carried over from the legacy app; record this as a config default).
- Detection **asks** before adding (S5). It produces a `recurring.candidate` that the review queue surfaces. "Not recurring" stores a suppression decision for the merchant.
- **Mark as recurring** (S14) on any transaction: type (bill, income, subscription, payment plan), interval or calendar rule, amount (fixed, or variable with tolerance), next expected date and category. It attaches matching history and bypasses the threshold. This is how annual bills enter.
- Tests: `TestS14_DetectMedianGap`, `TestS14_ThresholdConfigurable`, `TestS14_MarkAsRecurringBypassesThreshold`, `TestS5_DetectionAsksBeforeAdding`.

### [ ] M4.4 bills (`internal/bills`)
- **Reserve amount** (S5): last actual, else the most recent known amount, else the expected amount. The reserve feeds `Lines.Bills`.
- **New bill:** confirming it raises fixed immediately (a user-made config change), which reduces the pot first, then spend_base, through recompute. Config versions are effective-dated (S17).
- **Ended bill:** after 2 intervals with no occurrence, ask "still active?". A cancelled bill frees its reserve into the pot (through recompute; no special code).
- **Price change:** an amount outside tolerance is flagged, and the reserve updates to the last actual.
- **Missing bill:** alert past grace (alert plumbing comes in M9; record a `bill.missing` signal now).
- **Overage and underage:** handled in the engine (4g and section 5). An `OverageSource` event lets the user choose a source other than spending (S5).
- **Sinking envelopes** (S5): annual and quarterly use amount divided by months in the period; fixed intervals use `amount * 30.4375 / interval_days`; the first entry catches up with amount divided by months until due. Balances persist with no sweep; an underfunded charge is an overage.
- **Bills gap** (S5): covering an overspend from the bills reserve creates a gap shown with the next due bill, which flows as an overage when the bill posts (engine 4g and 4h).
- **Partial payments** both count. Card bills count on the date charged to the card (S5).
- Tests: `TestS5_ReserveUsesLastActual`, `TestS5_NewBillReducesPotThenBase`, `TestS5_CancelledBillFreesIntoPot`, `TestS5_PriceChangeFlagged`, `TestS5_SinkingSetAsideFormulas`, `TestS5_SinkingCatchUp`, `TestS5_SinkingPersistsNoSweep`, `TestS5_OverageFromSpendingByDefault`, `TestS5_UnderageSweepsToSavings`, `TestS5_BillsGapFlowsAsOverage`.

### [ ] M4.5 subscriptions and payment plans
- Subscriptions (S6, A-004): their own category bucket, reserved like bills, the same detection, price-change and missing rules, and underage sweeps. Cutting one frees its reserve into the pot.
- Payment plans (S19.9): a finite-occurrence schedule with total, installment and remaining count, detected from the first installment. Remaining installments count in the bills reserve; completion frees the reserve into the pot; early payoff is supported; the category comes from the originating purchase.
- Tests: `TestS6_SubscriptionChargeUsesReserveNotPool`, `TestS6_CancelFreesIntoPot`, `TestS19_9_PaymentPlanCompletesAndFrees`.

### [ ] M4.6 forecasts and extra decomposition
- Expected occurrences per month from schedules. Months with extras are labeled ("extra paycheck") (S14).
- "Next month's plan so far: $X, expected $Y by schedule" on `/home` (S2 example A, S14).
- **Extra decomposition** (S2) for the pot: by stream, for example "salary: +1 occurrence (+$2000)". Default suggestions: an extra occurrence is one-time, a per-deposit increase is permanent, a lump is one-time. Suggestions only; never applied by themselves.
- Tests: `TestS2_ExampleA_ExtraOccurrenceLabeled`, `TestS2_DecompositionSuggestsButNeverApplies`, `TestS14_NextPlanSoFarForecast`.
