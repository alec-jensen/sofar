# M10 extended systems

Spec: S19.9 (loans), S19.12, S19.13, S19.14, S19.21, S19.22, S19.23, S19.24 (pending polish). Architecture: A3 (platform tables), A9.3 invariants 8 and 9.

**Goal:** net worth and investments, multi-currency, the debt planner and loans, trips, the tax module, soft targets, and finished pending handling.

**Exit:** feature tests plus invariants 8 and 9 pass. Refine the tasks at the start of the milestone.

---

### [ ] M10.1 net worth, assets, holdings (S19.13)
- Net worth = assets minus liabilities, with daily history from balance snapshots, broken down by account type. Manual assets and liabilities with valuation history.
- **Holdings** come from the Bridge extension where present. **Never sum holdings on top of the account balance** (the balance already includes them). Prefer the reported total cost basis; otherwise shares times per-share price. Performance (gain and loss), allocation, and contributions versus growth.
- Dividends and interest landing in a budget account are income. Reinvested amounts never touch the plan.
- A pluggable price source for manually tracked holdings.
- `known-facts.md`: one retirement account reports `0.00`. Show the balance date and flag accounts whose holdings disagree with the balance.
- Tests: `TestS19_13_NoDoubleCountHoldings`, `TestS19_13_CostBasisPreference`, `TestS19_13_ReinvestedNotIncome`.

### [ ] M10.2 multi-currency (S19.14)
- Each account has its own currency. The plan is computed in the home currency. Rates come from **Frankfurter** (a network call allowed by CLAUDE.md, through `internal/netguard`, with a daily cache in `fx_rates`); weekends and holidays use the last available rate; the source is pluggable.
- Foreign spending uses the bank-charged amount. FX fees are detected (category `fees_interest`). Net worth revalues at current rates.
- Tests: `TestS19_14_WeekendUsesLastRate`, `TestS19_14_BankChargedAmountAuthoritative`, `TestA9_Inv8_ForeignIncomeCountedOnce`.

### [ ] M10.3 debt planner and loans (S19.12, S19.9)
- Debts from liability accounts and loans (APR, minimum, balance, provider or manual). Strategies: avalanche, snowball, custom. Simulated payoff date and interest, with scenario comparison.
- Extra payments are funded from a pot allocation or a standing line, integrated with the debt payoff goal.
- Loans: an amortization schedule from rate and term, split into interest and principal. Loan payments are bills, and extra principal is the debt payoff goal (S13).
- Tests: `TestS19_12_AvalancheVsSnowball`, `TestS19_9_AmortizationSplitsInterestPrincipal`.

### [ ] M10.4 trips (S19.21)
- A trip has a name, dates, destination, tag, an optional goal and an optional soft target. Auto-tag by date range and out-of-area clusters (S18a trips). Multi-currency aware, with a per-trip report. Travel mode switches display to local currency and time, and the UI explains when the display zone differs from the calculation zone (S3).
- Tests: `TestS19_21_AutoTagByDateRange`, `TestS3_TravelDisplayZoneMessage`.

### [ ] M10.5 tax module (S19.22, optional)
- A tax set-aside standing line equal to a user-set percentage of **last month's** self-employment income (it feeds `Lines.TaxSetAside`). Quarterly reminder dates, a "Taxes" category, deductible tags, a year-end export, and an optional mileage log. Informational only.
- Tests: `TestS19_22_SetAsideFromPreviousMonthSelfEmployment`.

### [ ] M10.6 soft targets (S19.23)
- Per category, group, tag or trip, monthly or rolling. Progress bars, threshold alerts, history, and a suggested target from the trailing 3-month average. **Never** an input to `ComputeMonth`: add `TestS19_23_SoftTargetsNeverChangeMath`, which hashes `MonthState` with and without targets.
