# M9 reports, alerts, forecasting, search, splits, receipts

Spec: S19.1, S19.2, S19.3, S19.4, S19.6, S19.8, S19.10, S19.11 (goal projections), S19.20 (reports, dashboard cards, glance). Architecture: A7 (alerts and hourly jobs), A9.3 invariant 9.

**Goal:** the cash timeline and shortfall alerts, data health and drift, the full alert catalog with channels, search and bulk edit, receipts and itemization, recaps and time travel, and subscription intelligence.

**Exit criteria:**
- Simulated ledgers produce correct forecasts, drift flags and alerts.
- The alert catalog tests pass.
- Invariant 9 passes with receipts-driven splits.

Refine these tasks into subtasks at the start of the milestone (README section 1).

---

### [ ] M9.1 cash timeline and credit card float (S19.1)
- A projected balance per budget account for the next 60 to 90 days, starting from the latest balance snapshot. It applies expected bills, subscriptions, sinking charges, scheduled income, payment-plan installments, pending items and card payments due. Uncertain amounts show a range from past variability.
- Track statement balance, current balance, due date and minimum due per card. If the provider lacks them, the user enters them. The cash check on home (S10) subtracts card payments due.
- **Shortfall alert** when below the user's buffer (default $0), naming the earliest day and the cause.
- Tests: `TestS19_1_TimelineAppliesSchedulesAndCardDue`, `TestS19_1_ShortfallNamesDayAndCause`, `TestS10_CashCheckAmberLine`.

### [ ] M9.2 plan forecast and what-if (S19.1)
- The next plan so far, plus a 12-month projection from schedules and trailing history.
- **Sandbox:** scenarios (lose income, a raise, a new bill, a big purchase, an extra-paycheck month) run `ComputeMonth` on in-memory copies, compared side by side. They never write.
- Tests: `TestS19_1_SandboxNeverWrites`, `TestS19_1_ExtraPaycheckScenario`.

### [ ] M9.3 data health (S19.2)
- **Drift detection** (read-only): the reported balance at its balance date versus the ledger-implied balance since an anchor. A mismatch gets a probable cause (missing transaction, duplicate, pending, id rotation) and one-tap fixes (a reconciliation adjustment or resolving the cause).
- **Assigned vs held:** the sum of virtual envelopes versus money in budget and tracked accounts. Read-only.
- A **data health page**: coverage, gaps, duplicates, healed ids, backlog, failing connections, stale accounts, rule health and accuracy.
- The **hidden pending estimate** (shared with M7.4), duplicate connection detection, credit utilization, negative-balance flags.
- Tests: `TestS19_2_DriftOnSimulatedGap`, `TestS19_2_DriftOnDuplicate`, `TestS19_2_AssignedVsHeldNeverChangesMath`.

### [ ] M9.4 alerts and insights (S19.3)
- An `alerts` catalog: every alert in S19.3, each with toggle, threshold, channel, quiet hours and digest grouping. Defaults push only the five S16 alerts.
- Channels: web push, email (SMTP), ntfy, Gotify, Pushover, Telegram, Matrix, Discord, a generic signed webhook, and the in-app inbox. One `notify.Channel` interface, with every channel tested against an `httptest` server. Outbound requests go through `internal/netguard`, but user-configured self-hosted endpoints (ntfy, Gotify) may need private addresses: add an explicit per-channel "allow private address" setting, off by default.
- **Insights feed:** category versus trailing average, subscription creep, pace versus plan, savings rate, merchant concentration and anomalies. Each is ranked, dismissable and snoozable, with a "why". A weekly digest.
- Tests: one `TestS19_3_Alert_<name>` per catalog entry, plus `TestS19_3_QuietHoursAndOnePerDay` and `TestS19_3_DefaultsPushOnlyFive`.

### [ ] M9.5 search, views and bulk edit (S19.4)
- A structured query language (field filters, amount ranges, dates, text), saved searches and smart lists, saved views as dashboard cards.
- **Bulk edit:** recategorize, tag, hide, link, mark reviewed, change month, split. Every bulk operation is one event and undoable.
- Command palette and full keyboard navigation (web).
- Tests: `TestS19_4_QueryParserFuzz`, `TestS19_4_BulkEditUndoable`.

### [ ] M9.6 splits, tags, receipts, itemization (S19.6)
- Splits are already first-class (M1.5). Add the rule action `split`, learned split suggestions ("costco: 70/30 groceries and household"), per-part links, and refunds allocated across parts.
- **Tags:** free-form or typed (trip, tax-deductible, reimbursable, project), learned like categories, usable in rules and reports.
- **Receipts:** image or PDF upload, stored with configurable retention. OCR through the optional sidecar (a self-hosted OCR engine); without it, manual entry. Auto-match a transaction by amount, date and merchant. Propose splits from line items.
- **Order itemization:** import an Amazon order CSV (and optionally forwarded emails), match charges (one charge may combine several orders), and itemize into splits with a learned item-to-category map.
- Tests: `TestA9_Inv9_SplitsSumAndNoDoubleCount` (rapid), `TestS19_6_RefundAllocatedAcrossParts`, `TestS19_6_ReceiptAutoMatch`, `TestS19_6_OrderCSVItemizes`.

### [ ] M9.7 recaps and time travel (S19.8)
- **As-of view:** reconstruct a month as of a past date from the event log, and diff it against now.
- **Month recap** at rollover: plan, extras and allocations, overspend and covers, saved, EF percentage, goals, absorption, categorization accuracy, biggest changes. Exportable as PDF or image. A **year in review**.
- Tests: `TestS19_8_AsOfReconstructsPastState`, `TestS19_8_RecapMatchesSnapshot`.

### [ ] M9.8 subscription intelligence and goal projections (S19.10, S19.11)
- Annualized cost, price history, renewal calendar, duplicate-service detection.
- **Trials:** marked or detected from a small first charge, with an alert before conversion.
- **Cancellation tracker** (verify no further charges) and a user-maintained cancel guide.
- **Negotiation toolkit:** talking points and a letter template generated locally, and outcome tracking.
- Goal projections with what-if contributions and milestones.
- Tests: `TestS19_10_TrialAlertBeforeConversion`, `TestS19_10_CancelledButChargedAlert`.

### [ ] M9.9 reports and dashboard (S19.20)
- Spending by category, group, merchant and tag over time; month over month; income versus spending; net cash flow; savings rate; plan history (plan, pot, spend_base, absorption ledger); a cash-flow sankey; pace versus plan; subscription and trip reports.
- A custom report builder with saved reports. Export as CSV, PDF and PNG.
- A customizable dashboard with the card list from S19.20.
- The **glance endpoint** `GET /api/v1/glance` (read-only hero, per day and review count, via a scoped token from M11).
- Tests: `TestS19_20_PlanHistoryMatchesSnapshots`, `TestS19_20_GlanceReadOnly`.
