# M7 transfers, refunds, reimbursements

Spec: S12, S12a, S12b, S13 (card payments), S19.24 (pending links). Architecture: A3 (reimbursements), A5 (matcher), A9.3 invariants 7 and 14. ADR-0007.

**Goal:** neutral transfers, refund and reimbursement links that net in the expense's month, receivables, held payments, and a matcher that abstains when unsure and learns patterns safely.

**Exit criteria:**
- Every S12a acceptance test (1 to 12) and S12b acceptance test (1 to 7) passes, including **the reported bug** as a named scenario.
- Invariants 7 and 14 pass (remove the skip added in M5).
- Worked example F passes (remove its skip).
- Auto-tentative precision is at least 99% in simulation.

---

### [x] M7.1 transfers and card payments (S12, S13)
- **Auto-pair:** opposite sign, equal amount, between linked accounts, within 3 days, using the transaction dates. The pair is neutral and still shown in review. Learn account pairs.
- **One-sided transfers** ask: neutral, savings or spending. A contribution to an investment account defaults to a **savings transfer** (category `investments`).
- **Card payments:** to a linked card, a transfer; to an unlinked card, spending (recategorizable).
- Port the pairing idea from `git show main:server/suggest.go` (`pairTransfers`), changing it to these rules.
- Tests: `TestS12_AutoPairWithinThreeDays`, `TestS12_OneSidedTransferAsks`, `TestS12_InvestmentContributionIsSavingsTransfer`, `TestS13_CardPaymentLinkedIsTransfer`, `TestS13_CardPaymentUnlinkedIsSpending`.

### [x] M7.2 links and netting (S12)
- `links` (A3): many-to-many, partial amounts, state `tentative` or `confirmed`, `created_by` (user, auto_receivable, auto_pattern), and optionally a split part.
- **Netting:** in recompute assembly, an outflow's amount is net of its links (confirmed **and** tentative, S12a "tentative links net immediately"), in the **expense's** month, in either order. A link never exceeds the expense's remaining amount unless the user overrides. **Overpayment** excess asks, defaulting to income (other).
- Refunds, chargebacks and reversals (negatives) use the same mechanism. A refund to a from-discretionary purchase returns money to discretionary (engine).
- **Unmatched reimbursement:** held outside income for 30 days, then asks.
- Tests: `TestS2_ExampleF_ReimbursementAcrossMonths`, `TestS12_RefundNetsInExpenseMonth`, `TestS12_ReimbursementBeforeExpenseAllowed`, `TestS12_OverpaymentExcessAsks`, `TestA9_Inv7_LinksNeverExceedRemainingNorCreateIncome`, `TestA9_Inv14_RejectTentativeRevertsExactly`.

### [x] M7.3 receivables (S12a)
- Fields and statuses as in the spec (`expected`, `partially received`, `received`, `written off`; `overdue` is derived).
- Created by: one tap on an expense ("dad owes me $11"), the split-with-someone flow, confirming a suggestion, or a rule action.
- Counts in full until money lands. Optional **treat as netted now**, then write-off if it is never paid (undoable).
- Aging alerts at 14, 30 and 60 days, and later personalized per payer (M7.6).
- Quick actions: "paid me back", "mark received" (a manual transaction, merged later per S11), unlink with undo, write off.
- Tests: `TestS12a_ReceivableCountsInFullUntilReceived`, `TestS12a_NettedNowThenWriteOff`, `TestS12a_E5_TwoInstallments`, `TestS12a_E6_OnePaymentTwoExpenses`, `TestS12a_E8_AgingAlerts`, `TestS12a_E3_CashMarkReceived`.

### [x] M7.4 held payments and late matching (S12a)
- `held_payments` state machine: `awaiting_expense` (grace default 5 days, later learned per account from posting lag), then `unlinked_reimbursement` (30-day hold), then asks.
- **Late match:** a trigger on every new or newly posted expense checks the held payments. A candidate must fit merchant type, have an amount at or above the payback, and have a transaction date at or before the payment. A candidate posting after the payment is **not penalized** up to the account's posting delay.
- **User declaration:** "this was for something not posted yet", with hints that create a placeholder which binds when the expense appears.
- **Hidden pending hint** (S12a item 4, S19.2): a persistent gap between balance and available balance not explained by visible items raises the abstain bias only. It never links.
- **Pending links:** a payback can link tentatively to a visible pending expense. The link carries over on posting (via the M2.4 reconcile), is re-evaluated if the pending item vanishes or changes amount, and returns to `expected` on dissolve.
- Tests: `TestS12a_HeldThenAsksAfterGrace`, `TestS12a_LateMatchSuggestsWhenExpensePosts`, `TestS12a_DeclarationBindsPlaceholder`, `TestS12a_HiddenPendingHintNeverLinks`, `TestS12a_E12_PendingInboundCancelledDissolves`.

### [x] M7.5 counterparties (S12b)
- Counterparties with fuzzy aliases, merged across platforms; merge and split; a policy (always reimbursement, never, ask); and optional auto-net, off by default.
- Profiles: reimbursement versus other share, lag distribution, share ratio, categories and merchants, rounding habits, reliability.
- Counterparty names come from descriptions (`known-facts.md`: `Transfer from <LAST>,<FIRST> <MI>`). Store them, never log them.
- Tests: `TestS12b_E3_AliasMergeAcrossPlatforms`, `TestS12b_E4_HandleChangeHeals`, `TestS12b_E9_MaskedNamesDisambiguated`.

### [x] M7.6 matcher (`internal/reimburse`)
- **Candidate generation** (in SQL): open receivables from the payer (or from unknown payers), plus expenses with remaining amount in the lookback window (30 days by default, payer-specific once learned). Same currency only.
- **Pair features** (S12b pipeline step 2; memo and description similarity includes emoji tokens as features, and an empty memo contributes nothing), and a calibrated scorer with the starter prior (round amounts, nearby dates, food categories) for **suggestions only**.
- **Global assignment:** min-cost bipartite matching with capacities (Hungarian algorithm), plus an **ambiguity gate** when the best assignment barely beats the second.
- **Three-way decision:** auto-tentative **only** for (1) an exact receivable match or (2) a proven pattern (3 consecutive confirmations, no rejections, confidence above threshold, amount at or below the $100 materiality gate). Otherwise suggest or abstain.
- Patterns: induction from 3 consistent pairs, probation, decay, one strike to demote, auto-demotion on precision drop with an owner notification.
- Expense-side prediction ("likely reimbursed by dad, about $11 within 3 days") as a one-tap receivable suggestion. Personalized aging.
- **Matching debugger** replays any payment: candidates, features, scores, assignment, and why it won or abstained. `link_events` and `pair_feature_snapshots` make every decision reproducible.
- Training signals exactly as in S12b, from user labels only.
