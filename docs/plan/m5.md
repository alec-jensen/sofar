### [ ] M5.2 general savings (S7)
- Config: fixed cents or basis points of the effective plan, effective-dated. No floor; a percentage falls with the plan.
- Planned credit at month start. **Verification:** a matching transfer into a linked savings account (a savings-transfer outflow or a deposit-type inflow) marks the month verified; "I moved it" does the same by hand. Unverified at month end is flagged.
- Tests: `TestS7_PercentFollowsPlan`, `TestS7_VerifiedByMatchingTransfer`, `TestS7_IMovedIt`, `TestS7_UnverifiedFlagged`.

### [ ] M5.3 goals (S7)
- Goal: name, target, and either a monthly amount or a date (the other is derived), plus priority and paused. Each goal gets its own savings category (S1a, the "Goals" group).
- **Completed:** the line stops, and the app asks where the freed contribution goes (default: the pot, then savings). **Overfunded:** the excess goes to general savings.
- **Purchase** linked to a goal draws from the envelope up to its balance (engine 4g), the excess counts as spending, the remaining balance asks where to go, and the goal closes.
- **Abandoned:** asks where the balance goes. **Borrowed from:** always shown, with the choice of a repay standing line, slipping the date, or reducing the target.
- **Date-based goal behind schedule:** the user chooses to raise the monthly amount or let the date slip; nothing adjusts on its own. Approaching or missed deadlines raise signals for M9 alerts.
- Tests: `TestS7_DateGoalDerivesMonthly`, `TestS7_MonthlyGoalDerivesDate`, `TestS7_CompletedAsksWhere`, `TestS7_OverfundToSavings`, `TestS7_PurchaseDrawsEnvelopeExcessIsSpending`, `TestS7_BorrowedShownWithChoices`, `TestS7_PausedStopsLine`.

### [ ] M5.4 emergency fund (S8)
- Target: `N * mean(trailing 3 months of actual bills + spending)`, N=3 by default, or a fixed override; show the percent funded. A target change is not a withdrawal.
- Funding: its own standing line, one-off pot allocations, and an optional opening balance.
- **Use:** a cover from the EF previews its impact, and on confirm creates a **rebuild line** over N months (the user picks N, suggested 6) that redirects general savings first (A-007). The line can be edited or deleted.
- Excess above target goes to general savings.
- Tests: `TestS8_TargetFromTrailingOutflow`, `TestS8_TargetChangeNotWithdrawal`, `TestS8_CoverCreatesRebuildLine`, `TestS8_RebuildRedirectsSavingsFirst`, `TestS8_ExcessToSavings`.

### [ ] M5.5 pot allocation and standing lines (S2)
- `POST /api/v1/months/{ym}/allocations` with splits `[{dest, amount, permanent}]`. Destinations: spending, general savings, a goal, the EF, discretionary.
- Permanent creates a **standing line** (effective from that month) or raises spend_base (engine 4c).
- **Convert** a past one-time allocation to a standing line.
- **"Make permanent?"**: when the pot repeats at the same size two months in a row, suggest it, with the previous split pre-filled as a suggestion only.
- **Absorption restore:** when the pot reappears after absorption, pre-fill "restore $X to spending", tracked against the absorption ledger (example D).
- **Take-back** when late data shrinks the pot (engine 4e).
- **Manual spend_base edit** (A-001 pin) and standing line edits are never auto-reversed, and show "overcommitted by $X".
- Tests: `TestS2_PermanentAllocationCreatesStandingLine`, `TestS2_ConvertOneTimeToStanding`, `TestS2_MakePermanentSuggestedOnRepeat`, `TestS2_RestoreSuggestionAfterAbsorption`, `TestS2_TakeBackFromDiscretionaryThenSavings`, `TestS2_ManualEditPinsAndShowsOvercommitted`.

### [ ] M5.6 overspend flow (S10)
- **Prompt creation:** when **posted** outflows cross `spend_pool + discretionary`. Only for the current and previous month (S3). Several in a day collapse into one card, and each is resolvable on its own. Push and in-app after a sync (push sending is wired in M8 and M9; emit the signal now).
- `POST /api/v1/overspends/{id}/covers` with `[{source, amount}]`. Sources: next month, general savings, a goal, the EF, the bills reserve, leave uncovered (A-008: never discretionary). Splits are allowed and there is no cap.
- **Settle** for uncovered debt.
- **Reassigning** an auto-carry later refunds the carry (engine section 5.4).
- Older months show a passive marker with a manual "resolve" button (S3).
- Tests: `TestS10_PromptOnlyForPostedCurrentAndPrevious`, `TestS10_CoverSourcesAllWork`, `TestS10_ReassignAutoCarryRefunds`, `TestS10_SettleUncovered`, `TestS3_OldMonthNoPrompt`.

### [ ] M5.7 rollover job (S15, S17, S16)
- `rollover` runs at 00:05 home time on the 1st, keyed `rollover:2026-11`. It marks the new month dirty, recomputes, and sends the **digest**: "october plan: $X (+/- $Y vs last month), uncategorized: $Z."
- It catches up on startup after downtime, and is idempotent and derived (late data recomputes the effects, because sweeps live in the engine).
- The server alone may: sweep leftovers, default the pot to savings, and carry unresolved overspend (S16). It never picks other cover sources and never edits goals.
- Tests: `TestS15_RolloverCatchUpAfterDowntime`, `TestS15_RolloverIdempotentByKey`, `TestS16_ServerNeverChoosesCoverSource`.
