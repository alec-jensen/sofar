### 2026-10-05: M7 transfers, links, receivables, counterparties, matcher (Claude Sonnet 5.5)
- Milestone M7 is built on `rebuild`, except S12a acceptance test 12 (every scheduled pull sends `pending=1`), which belongs to the sync client and lands with M2 (a named, skipped stub is in `internal/reimburse/s12a_test.go`). Migrations 00011 to 00014.
- `internal/transfers`: auto-pairs opposite equal rows in two accounts within 3 days (5 for an account pair seen 3 times), as system overlay events that undo like any other; a linked card payment is `credit_card_payment`, a pair with an investment account is `investments` (savings), an unlinked card payment stays spending; lone transfer-looking rows ask after 3 days (neutral, savings or spending, with savings pre-selected for investment names). Accounts gained a `subtype`.
- `internal/links`: many-to-many links with partial amounts and split parts, tentative and confirmed, created by user, auto_receivable or auto_pattern. Netting happens in recompute assembly, in the expense's own month and in either order of arrival; tentative links net immediately; a payment's unlinked remainder is a deposit (held out of income until reviewed, which is how overpayment asks). Rejecting restores the month's input hash exactly. Example F and invariants 7 and 14 now pass (their skips are gone).
- `internal/receivables`: status is derived from links (expected, partially received, received, written off) so it cannot drift; counts in full until money lands unless netted now; write-off reverts the netting and can be undone; installments, one payment for two expenses, cash "mark received" as a manual transaction, aging at 14, 30 and 60 days, personalised per payer.
- `internal/counterparties`: fuzzy aliases (Last,First equals First Last, truncations, masked surnames), merge across platforms, split, policy (ask, always, never), auto-net; payer strings are stored, never logged.
- `internal/reimburse`: receivables first; candidates scored by a starter prior (clean fraction, round amount, lag, expense kind, memo and emoji, payer history and category, a second payback on the same expense is heavily penalised); a hard evidence gate so an amount-and-date-only candidate is never even suggested (the reported bug); a min-cost assignment (Hungarian) across all open payments; an ambiguity gate; held payments (5 day grace, 30 day hold, then needs a decision) with late match on every new expense and user declarations that bind to one matching expense; pending links that carry over on posting and dissolve when an inbound item vanishes; the hidden-pending hint only raises the abstain bias; platform double entries are skipped; recurring allowances are asked about; pairs of expenses covered by one payment are suggested or, if several fit, asked about.
- Learning: patterns are induced from confirmed pairs only; three consecutive confirmations with no strike graduate a pattern; one rejection or unlink strikes it back to suggest-only; amounts above $100, payers with mixed history, ambiguity and anything without a proven pattern never auto-link; user-written patterns beat learned ones; bulk unlink retrains; patterns decay; precision below 80% demotes and emits an event for the owner. Every decision is logged with its features (`link_events`) and `GET /api/v1/debug/match/{txn}` replays it.
- API (all in `api/openapi.yaml`, contract-tested): `/owed`, receivables (create, received, write-off), `/payments/{txn}/candidates`, `/links` (create, confirm, reject, delete), `/counterparties`, `/debug/match/{txn}`, transfer asks and unpair, and undo and redo for link, receivable and counterparty events. A keyed `matching.run` job (after each import or manual entry) and a nightly job pair transfers, process payments and sweep held ones.
- Gates (A9.10): auto-tentative precision was 100% (and no false links) in the payback simulation over six seeds, but the sample is tiny (one automatic link). The simulated world is dense: dining purchases of similar size every day, so nearly every payback is genuinely ambiguous and correctly abstains. Coverage is therefore low there; treat the precision number as "the gate is never violated" rather than a measurement, and revisit with real data after the probe.
- Interpretations to confirm: an overpayment's excess is held out of income until the owner answers (the text says it defaults to income); a declared placeholder binds as a tentative link authored by the user when exactly one expense carries the hint; "payer known" means at least one confirmed payback or a policy of always.
