# sofar design

- lowercase throughout. calm, chill, plain-spoken. match the user's conversational style.
- keep copy short and useful. no slogans, finance jargon, motivational pressure, or repeated "little" phrases.
- minimal ui: quiet white cards, thin separators, restrained sage accents, readable text, and plenty of space.
- use rocket money's dashboard composition as the reference: left navigation; a clear money summary with a daily guide; a right rail for accounts and upcoming bills. on phones, bring accounts and upcoming bills near the top and use simple bottom navigation.
- use a quiet green header for the main number, rounded white sections, and readable spending totals. keep the data hierarchy and touch targets clear without copying rocket money's branding.
- keep sofar's own name, palette, and received-income budgeting rules. don't show predicted paychecks as available money.
- preserve the actual case of passwords, authenticator secrets, and user-entered values. lowercase presentation must never change credential values.

reference: https://www.rocketmoney.com/compare/ynab
